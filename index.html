Who We Are

AICODEV Ghana (Alternative Innovations For Community Development) is a non-profit organization based in Wa, Upper West Region, Ghana, focused on community development and research. The organization is dedicated to supporting deprived communities through various interventions, including health education, volunteer training, and social advocacy.

Our Mission & Vision

AICODEV Ghana works ‘to Change lives and build communities to have equal access to all Resources and social justice’.

A world of equal rights and access to all services for all

Compassionate Aid

Community Partnership

We work with local communities to develop sustainable solutions.

Transparent Impact

We measure and report on the outcomes of all our programs.

Global Reach

Our programs span across the Wa West Disrict in the Upper West Region of Ghana.

Thermatic Areas

Poverty Eradication and Livelihoods Empowerment

Poverty eradication and Livelihoods Empowerment constitute a critical, multifaceted thematic area in international development, aimed at shifting vulnerable households from dependency to self-reliance through, capacity building, asset accumulation, and financial inclusion.

EDUCATION

Focuses on improving access to quality education, promoting literacy, and ensuring inclusive learning opportunities for all children and adults.

WATER AND SANITATION

Water and sanitation is a critical thematic area, aiming for universal access to safe drinking water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH)

NUTRITION

Focuses on improving nutritional outcomes, especially for children and mothers, through targeted interventions and community-based programs.

Our Projects

Ongoing

Social Behaviour Change Communication to prevent Malaria in pregnancy through increasing IPTp uptake

Social and Behaviour Change Communication (SBCC) for Malaria in Pregnancy (MiP) increases Intermittent Preventive Treatment (IPTp) uptake by influencing knowledge, attitudes, and social norms among pregnant women, families, and health workers. Effective strategies include leveraging community role models, fostering male involvement, and promoting early Antenatal Care (ANC) to ensure at least three doses of SP.

Completed

Healthy Mothers, Smiling Babies Campaign Project

This task is critical. Tragically, based on currently published estimates, 260 000 women lose their life due to pregnancy or childbirth each year, while over 2 million babies die in their first month of life and around 2 million more are stillborn. That’s roughly 1 preventable death every 7 seconds. Based on current trends, a staggering 4 out of 5 countries are off track to meet targets for improving maternal survival by 2030. 1 in 3 will fail to meet targets for reducing newborn deaths. Women and families everywhere need high quality care that supports them physically and emotionally, before, during and after birth. Health systems must evolve to manage the many health issues that impact maternal and newborn health. These not only include direct obstetric complications but also mental health conditions, noncommunicable diseases and family planning.
